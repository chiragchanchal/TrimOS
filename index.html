Get Started in 60 Seconds

Effortless Installation

No root required. Automated installer detects your TV brand and sets up everything over your local Wi-Fi.

1

Enable ADB on your TV

Go to TV Settings → Device Preferences → About. Tap Build 7 times until you are a developer, then enable Network / USB Debugging.

2

Run the 1-Click Installer

Run the universal installer from your computer connected to the same Wi-Fi:

python trimos.py install --ip <TV_IP>

On Windows, you can simply double-click trimos.bat!

3

Enjoy Your New TV

Your TV will instantly launch into TrimOS. Enjoy smooth 60fps navigation, zero ads, ad-free SmartTube, and 32 themes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Does installing TrimOS void my smart TV warranty? ▼
No. TrimOS does not root your television or flash custom recovery partitions. It operates using standard Android pm disable-user commands that disable bloatware only in user space. Your factory firmware remains 100% untouched.
How do I revert back to the stock factory home screen? ▼
You can restore your original manufacturer launcher in seconds with one command: python trimos.py restore --ip <TV_IP>. This immediately re-enables your stock home screen (PatchWall, Google TV, etc.).
Will Netflix, Prime Video, and HDMI inputs still work? ▼
Yes, completely! TrimOS preserves all TV input switching, HDMI-CEC connections, audio return channels (ARC), and DRM Widevine L1 4K playback.
Does the Home button stay locked across cold reboots? ▼
Yes. TrimOS includes a built-in accessibility service (HomeKeyService) and boot receiver that locks the remote Home key to TrimOS across device restarts and power cycles.
